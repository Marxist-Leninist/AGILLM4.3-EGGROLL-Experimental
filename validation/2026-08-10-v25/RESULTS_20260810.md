# AGILLM-4.3 EGGROLL validation — 10 August 2026

## Decision

The experiments found genuine antithetic fitness signal in AGILLM-4.3's top-1 MoE routers, but **did not demonstrate faster pretraining**. The controlled fixed-token active branch took **41.7% longer** than the mean baseline training window. Its mean local-loss delta was `-0.0007857`, but it beat both baseline repeats on only 3 of 14 commits and lost to both on 5.

Therefore v25 is published as a **default-off experimental sidecar**. Production v22 was not changed or restarted, and no long-running EGGROLL checkpoint fork was started.

## Initial population test

At the original global sigma `0.0003`, both population 8 and population 16 produced no measurable pair signal on 13 of 14 events. A larger population could not rescue perturbations that almost never changed the selected expert.

## Reproducibility gate

Exact token batches and block/objective schedules matched across replay branches, but independent CUDA processes still reached a maximum local-loss difference of `0.05799999999999983` and anchor-CE difference of `2.8916000000000004`. The active branches were blocked rather than compared against a falsely exact baseline.

## Unified v25

`agillm43_singlefile_intelligence_v25_eggroll_unified_experimental.py`  
SHA-256: `8b9e62eaa9dde68c624ea9f3d4c9311b4a841ec43a89761453754e0673e25674`

v25 combines the two previous experimental branches:

- strict pre-tokenised data with file and integer-token-stream SHA-256 receipts;
- per-router sigma maps;
- measurable-improvement acceptance;
- route-flip, router-margin and perturbation-logit diagnostics;
- exact rollback and update-RMS clipping;
- checkpoint/config compatibility checks;
- default-off behaviour and dry-run support.

## Recommended next use

Use the included launcher in dry-run mode only. Promotion to weight-changing mode requires a longer matched experiment that shows both lower held-out loss and improvement per GPU-hour.

Fine-tuning and pretraining are both optimisation, so EGGROLL could help either in principle. The present result is narrower: this router-side implementation found real signal, but its extra population forwards did not repay their runtime cost on this model and hardware.
